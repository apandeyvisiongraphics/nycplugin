### 12. H2: Common Questions About Custom Signs in NYC → `faq` (existing markup/CSS)

- `p` <strong>How much do custom signs cost in NYC?</strong>
- `p` Most business signs cost between $300 and $20,000 installed. Window graphics sit at the low end, while illuminated channel letters and large building signs sit at the high end. NYC permits, access equipment, and landlord requirements can add cost. We itemize every quote from a photo or a site survey.
- `p` <strong>How long does a custom sign take?</strong>
- `p` Simple graphics take one to two weeks. Lobby and office signs usually take two to three weeks. Illuminated exterior signs take three to five weeks, plus permit review. Landmark districts and building approvals can add time, so we flag them at the start of your project.
- `p` <strong>Do I need a permit for a business sign in NYC?</strong>
- `p` Usually yes. New York City requires a permit for business signs over six square feet and for any illuminated sign. Attached signs can need an alteration permit, lit signs need an electrical permit, and landmark districts add review. Ask any sign company in New York you compare who files the paperwork. We prepare it and coordinate the licensed filer.
- `p` <strong>Who makes the sign?</strong>
- `p` Our production team builds your sign to the drawing you approve, and we check each piece before it ships. Specialty items can involve trusted partners. We tell you before you approve the quote.
- `p` <strong>Who installs the sign?</strong>
- `p` A licensed NYC sign hanger installs your sign, because the city requires one for most installations. A licensed electrician makes the final connection on lit signs. We tell you who will be on site before installation day.
- `p` <strong>Can you provide a certificate of insurance for our building?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We provide a certificate of insurance on request, and we can send it before work begins. Buildings set their own coverage wording, so send us your requirements early, and we will match them where we can.
- `p` <strong>Can you install after hours or on weekends?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We schedule installs around your building's rules, including evenings, weekends, and freight elevator windows. After-hours work can add labor charges, and we confirm that in your quote.
- `p` <strong>Can you remove our old sign?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We remove the old sign as part of the install plan. NYC requires a licensed sign hanger for removal, and landmark buildings can require review. Any wall repair is scoped in your quote.
- `p` <strong>Can you handle signs for several NYC locations?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We handle one address or many, with the same drawings, finishes, and schedule across every site. Tell us each building's address, and we confirm permits and installation for each one.
- `p` <strong>Do you serve all five boroughs?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We serve all five boroughs, and every building has its own rules and access needs. Tell us your address when you request a quote, and we plan permits, scheduling, and installation for your site.
