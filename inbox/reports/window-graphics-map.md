### 7. H2: NYC Rules for Window Graphics → `features-grid` (new section template)

- `p` Window graphics sit under a few city rules. The rules below are the city's, and they may change.
- `h3` <strong>Coverage Limits</strong>
- `p` In landmark districts, LPC limits vinyl on storefront glass to 20 percent of the glazed area, subject to staff approval. Some zoning districts cap window coverage too.
- `h3` <strong>Permits and Zoning</strong>
- `p` A non-lit sign of about six square feet or less may need no DOB permit, and zoning rules still apply. Larger or lit graphics may need review.
- `h3` <strong>Landlord Approval</strong>
- `p` Your lease may set window rules, and your landlord or building management may need to approve the design before work starts. Send any lease sign criteria early, and we will prepare the drawings they ask for.
- `p` We can provide a certificate of insurance upon request, and our team will confirm your building's requirements.
- `h3` <strong>Restaurant Grade Card</strong>
- `p` The NYC letter grade card must stay visible to passersby and cannot be hidden. We plan graphics around it.
- `h3` <strong>Glass Door Markings</strong>
- `p` Decals on glass doors may double as the marking the state requires on doors and nearby sidelights. See <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/window-film/">window film in NYC</a></strong> for the marking heights.
- `p` Permit paperwork is part of the plan our project lead manages. Ask <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">our team</a></strong> what applies at your address.

### 13. H2: Common Questions → `faq` (existing markup/CSS)

- `p` <strong>How much do storefront window graphics cost?</strong>
- `p` Cost depends on size, material, and coverage. Pricing ranges per square foot are about $5 to $15 for cut vinyl lettering, $12 to $25 for printed decals, and $15 to $30 for perforated film. Installation scope is stated in your quote, and your quote replaces these ranges.
- `p` <strong>Can customers still see out from inside with perforated window film?</strong>
- `p` Yes, in daylight from inside. Small holes in the film let you see out while the printed face shows to the street. The effect reverses at night when interior lights are brighter than the street, so the hole pattern and your lighting both matter.
- `p` <strong>How long do window graphics last outdoors?</strong>
- `p` It depends on the film, the sun on the glass, and which side the graphic sits, so your quote names the material and how long it suits your window.
- `p` <strong>Can you swap graphics for seasonal promotions?</strong>
- `p` Yes. Tell us the display dates, and we plan the material and the swap around them. In a landmark district, replacing vinyl that LPC already approved may not require a new permit, and <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">our team</a></strong> confirms what applies at your address.
- `p` <strong>Can you remove old graphics or lettering first?</strong>
- `p` Yes. Removal is part of the install plan, and our team confirms the removal scope in your quote. Existing film, adhesive, and glass condition affect the work, so send a photo of the old graphics.
- `p` <strong>What is the usual turnaround?</strong>
- `p` Most window graphics run about 1 to 2 weeks from approved drawing to installation, plus shipping from Salt Lake City. Larger full-coverage jobs and building approvals can add time. Your quote confirms the schedule, and our project lead manages it.
- `p` <strong>Do window graphics need a permit in NYC?</strong>
- `p` Often not for small, non-lit signs of about six square feet or less, and zoning rules still apply. Larger or lit graphics may need review. LPC limits and your lease may add rules, and <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">our team</a></strong> confirms what applies at your address.
- `p` <strong>Can a restaurant cover the window where the grade card goes?</strong>
- `p` Leave it clear. The NYC letter grade card must stay visible to passersby and cannot be hidden, so we plan graphics around it. Send a photo of your entrance, and we will show the card position on the drawing.
- `p` <strong>What is the difference between window graphics and window film?</strong>
- `p` Window graphics are cut, printed, or perforated vinyl that carry a message. Window film is a frosted or decorative treatment for privacy. Both go on existing glass, and <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">our team</a></strong> can help you choose between them.
