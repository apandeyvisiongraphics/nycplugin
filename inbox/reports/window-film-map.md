### 6. H2: Glass Door Markings and Frosted Bands → `features-grid` (new section template)

- `p` Frosted bands often double as glass door markings. The rules below are New York State's and the city's, and they may change.
- `h3` <strong>Where the Rule Applies</strong>
- `p` New York State's Industrial Code covers transparent glass doors and the fixed glass beside them in commercial buildings. It reaches fixed sidelights within six feet of a door, and it does not cover all interior glass walls.
- `h3` <strong>Marking Heights</strong>
- `p` Marks sit in two zones: 30 to 36 inches and 60 to 66 inches above the floor. They must be opaque and easy to see from any direction. Some narrow sidelights may be exempt, and doors with horizontal separation bars may skip the upper zone.
- `h3` <strong>Who Confirms</strong>
- `p` Your building or design professional confirms what applies to your doors. We state the rule and show your chosen band heights on the drawing, and we do not certify compliance.
