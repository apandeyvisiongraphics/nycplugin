### 11. H2: Common Questions → `faq` (existing markup/CSS)

- `p` <strong>Do you remove the old sign?</strong>
- `p` Yes. Removal is part of the install plan, so the old sign comes down before the new one goes up. New York City requires a licensed sign hanger for most removals, and our licensed partners handle it. <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">Send us a photo of your old sign</a></strong>, and we will plan both steps.
- `p` <strong>Do you repair the wall?</strong>
- `p` Yes. Wall patching and paint touch-up are scoped in your quote, based on the photos you send. Old signs leave holes, marks, and adhesive, so the quote reflects your wall's condition. <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">Tell us about your wall</a></strong> and any finish requirements.
- `p` <strong>Can you install after hours?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We schedule installs around your building's rules, including evenings, weekends, and freight elevator windows. After-hours work can add labor charges, and we confirm them in your quote. Tell us your building's work hours when you request a quote.
- `p` <strong>Can you provide a certificate of insurance?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We can provide a certificate of insurance upon request and send it before work begins. Buildings set their own coverage wording, so <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">send us your requirements</a></strong> early. Our team will confirm what we can provide for your building.
- `p` <strong>Who does the installation?</strong>
- `p` Licensed installation partners install your sign, and a licensed electrician connects lit signs. A Vision Graphics project lead manages the schedule, building access, and paperwork from start to finish. We tell you who will be on site before installation day.
- `p` <strong>How long does it take from approval to install?</strong>
- `p` Simple graphics usually take one to two weeks, and lobby and office signs take two to three weeks. Illuminated exterior signs take three to five weeks, plus permit review. Shipping from our Salt Lake City facility adds transit time, which we confirm in your quote. Landmark districts and building approvals can add more.
- `p` <strong>Do you handle building management approval and permits?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We prepare the drawings and paperwork building management asks for, and interior signs usually depend on that approval. Exterior signs generally need a New York City DOB permit, and lit signs need a separate electrical permit. Landmark districts add review. We coordinate the licensed filer.
- `p` <strong>Do you install in all five boroughs?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We serve all five boroughs, and each building has its own access rules and approvals. Tell us your address when you request a quote, and we will plan the schedule, permits, and crew for your site.
