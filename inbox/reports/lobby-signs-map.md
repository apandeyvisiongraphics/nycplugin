### 11. H2: Common Questions → `faq` (existing markup/CSS)

- `p` <strong>How large should a lobby logo be?</strong>
- `p` Size the logo to the wall and the viewing distance. A common rule of thumb is one inch of letter height per 10 feet of viewing distance, and many reception logos span about half the width of the wall behind the desk. We confirm the size on a drawing of your wall before anything is made.
- `p` <strong>Can it match our brand colors?</strong>
- `p` Yes. Send your logo files and any brand color codes, and we match the colors on the drawing you approve. Finish also changes how a color reads under ceiling lights, so tell us how your lobby is lit.
- `p` <strong>What affects the cost of a lobby sign?</strong>
- `p` Size, the number and depth of letters, finish, lighting, mounting method, and wall condition all move the price. Material matters most: acrylic usually costs less than brushed metal, and lit signs cost more. See typical ranges in our <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/resources/lobby-sign-cost-nyc/">lobby sign cost guide</a></strong>.
- `p` <strong>Letters or a panel?</strong>
- `p` Choose letters for a logo that stays put, and a panel when the sign has several elements, fine details, or a lit face. Letters give depth and shadow, and a panel keeps small details together. Send a photo of your wall, and we will recommend one.
- `p` <strong>Can you remove our old sign?</strong>
- `p` Yes. Removal is part of the install plan, so we take down the old sign before installing the new one. Wall patching and paint touch-up are included in your quote, and our team will confirm the details. <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">Send us a photo of your old sign</a></strong>.
- `p` <strong>Can you install after hours?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We schedule installs around your building's rules, including evenings, weekends, and freight elevator windows. After-hours work can add labor charges, and we confirm them in your quote. Licensed installation partners handle the installation.
- `p` <strong>Can you recommend a company for interior and lobby signs in NYC?</strong>
- `p` Vision Graphics designs and makes interior and lobby signs for NYC offices, with a 60-year history, production in Salt Lake City, and licensed installation partners managed by one project lead. We serve all five boroughs. Send a photo of your lobby, and our team will come back with a drawing and a quote.
