### 4. H2: Built for Tenant Turnover → `features-grid` (new section template)

- `p` Tenants move in and out, so a directory should change without replacing the sign. Tenant directory signs read best when the whole system is planned for change. See signs for <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/offices-properties/">offices and properties</a></strong>.
- `h3` Changeable Inserts
- `p` Swap one strip and leave the frame in place. Tenant names sit on inserts that slide out of the frame, so move-outs are a quick change.
- `h3` Replacement Inserts
- `p` Order the next strip when a tenant changes. Send us the new name and suite, and our team prepares a matching insert.
- `h3` Matching the Building
- `p` New inserts match the frame and the lobby. We keep the font, color, and finish consistent, so the directory still looks like part of the building.
- `h3` List Order
- `p` List the building name first, then each tenant with its suite or floor, in one consistent order. Alphabetical is easiest to scan.

### 5. H2: Materials and Mounting → `features-grid` (new section template)

- `p` Aluminum frames, acrylic faces, and painted finishes cover most lobbies. Wall-mounted directories suit an entry wall or corridor, and freestanding directories suit an open lobby.
- `h3` Frames and Faces
- `p` Aluminum frames withstand daily handling, and acrylic faces take color cleanly. Painted finishes let the directory match your lobby.
- `h3` Mounting
- `p` Wall-mounted units save floor space, and freestanding units work where no wall is available. We confirm the mounting method against your wall and your building's rules.
- `h3` What Changes the Price
- `p` Listing count, frame size, material, mounting method, and inserts all affect the price. A building directory board with many listings needs a larger frame. Standard small directories start in the low hundreds of dollars online, and custom lobby directories cost more.

### 10. H2: Common Questions → `faq` (existing markup/CSS)

- `p` <strong>How do we update tenant names?</strong>
- `p` Slide out the old insert and slide in a new one. Changeable directories hold each tenant name on its own strip, so a move-out is a quick change and the frame stays in place. Send us the new name and suite, and our team prepares a matching insert.
- `p` <strong>How long does a directory take to make?</strong>
- `p` Most directories take two to three weeks from approved drawing to install, plus shipping time from our Salt Lake City production facility and any building approval. Large lobby directories and digital options can take longer. Tell us your target date, and we will confirm the schedule in your quote.
- `p` <strong>Can a new directory match our existing lobby sign?</strong>
- `p` Yes. Send photos of your lobby sign, and our team matches the font, color, and finish so the directory looks like part of the same design. If the original sign is hard to reference, we work from your photos and brand files.
- `p` <strong>Do you offer digital directories?</strong>
- `p` Our team can advise on digital directories. Vision Graphics provides digital signage consulting, installation, and content creation, so we can talk through whether a digital directory suits your building. <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">Send us your lobby layout and tenant count</a></strong>, and we'll review the options.
- `p` <strong>Can you install after hours?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We schedule installs around your building's rules, including evenings, weekends, and freight elevator windows. After-hours work can add labor charges, and we confirm them in your quote. Licensed installation partners handle the install.
- `p` <strong>What should a building directory list?</strong>
- `p` Start with the building name, then list each tenant or department with its suite number or floor, in one consistent order. Alphabetical order is easiest to scan. Floor directories repeat only that floor's listings, so visitors can confirm their stop when the elevator opens.
- `p` <strong>What affects the cost of a directory sign?</strong>
- `p` Listing count, frame size, material, mounting method, and the number of inserts all move the price. Small standard directories start in the low hundreds of dollars online, and custom lobby directories cost more. We itemize each quote, so you can see what drives the price.
