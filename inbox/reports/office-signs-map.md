### 11. H2: Common Questions → `faq` (existing markup/CSS)

- `p` <strong>What signs does an office need?</strong>
- `p` Most offices need four groups: a brand sign at reception, room ID signs, wayfinding signs and directories, and the safety signs your building requires. Plan them as one system so fonts, finishes, and mounting heights match. Your building and fit-out team sets exit, stair, and evacuation signs.
- `p` <strong>Can name signs be changed when staff moves?</strong>
- `p` Yes, when the sign has a replaceable name insert. Occupant names are exempt from the ADA sign rules, so you can change an insert while the room number stays in place. <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">Send us the new name</a></strong> and our team will prepare a matching insert.
- `p` <strong>Do room signs need to be ADA compliant?</strong>
- `p` Where room signs are provided, the ADA Standards require raised characters and Grade 2 Braille on signs that identify permanent rooms, such as room numbers, conference rooms, and restrooms, mounted 48 to 60 inches high on the latch side. Directional signs need visual rules only, and occupant names are exempt. See our <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/ada-signs/">ADA signs</a></strong> page.
- `p` <strong>How long does it take?</strong>
- `p` Most office signs take two to three weeks from approved drawing to installation, plus shipping time from our Salt Lake City production facility and any building approval. Large multi-floor packages can take longer. Tell us your move-in date, and we will confirm the schedule in your quote.
- `p` <strong>Can you work after hours?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We schedule installs around your building's rules, including evenings, weekends, and freight elevator windows. After-hours work can add labor charges, and we confirm them in your quote. Licensed installation partners handle the install.
- `p` <strong>Can you plan signs for several floors or suites?</strong>
- `p` Yes. We plan the whole set together, quote it by floor, and phase installs with your building so each floor is finished before the next begins. <strong><a href="https://www.visiondoesit.com/nyc/contact/">Send us your floor plans and room list</a></strong>, and our team will prepare one drawing set for each floor.
