10. H2: Common Questions

How do we update tenant names? 

Slide out the old insert and slide in a new one. Changeable directories hold each tenant name on its own strip, so a move-out is a quick change and the frame stays in place. Send us the new name and suite, and our team prepares a matching insert.

How long does a directory take to make? 

Most directories take two to three weeks from approved drawing to install, plus shipping time from our Salt Lake City production facility and any building approval. Large lobby directories and digital options can take longer. Tell us your target date, and we will confirm the schedule in your quote.

Can a new directory match our existing lobby sign? 

Yes. Send photos of your lobby sign, and our team matches the font, color, and finish so the directory looks like part of the same design. If the original sign is hard to reference, we work from your photos and brand files.

Do you offer digital directories? 

Our team can advise on digital directories. Vision Graphics provides digital signage consulting, installation, and content creation, so we can talk through whether a digital directory suits your building. Send us your lobby layout and tenant count, and we'll review the options.

Can you install after hours? 

Yes. We schedule installs around your building's rules, including evenings, weekends, and freight elevator windows. After-hours work can add labor charges, and we confirm them in your quote. Licensed installation partners handle the install.

What should a building directory list? 

Start with the building name, then list each tenant or department with its suite number or floor, in one consistent order. Alphabetical order is easiest to scan. Floor directories repeat only that floor's listings, so visitors can confirm their stop when the elevator opens.

What affects the cost of a directory sign? 

Listing count, frame size, material, mounting method, and the number of inserts all move the price. Small standard directories start in the low hundreds of dollars online, and custom lobby directories cost more. We itemize each quote, so you can see what drives the price.

11. H2: Common Questions

How large should a lobby logo be? 

Size the logo to the wall and the viewing distance. A common rule of thumb is one inch of letter height per 10 feet of viewing distance, and many reception logos span about half the width of the wall behind the desk. We confirm the size on a drawing of your wall before anything is made.

Can it match our brand colors? 

Yes. Send your logo files and any brand color codes, and we match the colors on the drawing you approve. Finish also changes how a color reads under ceiling lights, so tell us how your lobby is lit.

What affects the cost of a lobby sign? 

Size, the number and depth of letters, finish, lighting, mounting method, and wall condition all move the price. Material matters most: acrylic usually costs less than brushed metal, and lit signs cost more. See typical ranges in our lobby sign cost guide.

Letters or a panel? 

Choose letters for a logo that stays put, and a panel when the sign has several elements, fine details, or a lit face. Letters give depth and shadow, and a panel keeps small details together. Send a photo of your wall, and we will recommend one.

Yes. Removal is part of the install plan, so we take down the old sign before installing the new one. Wall patching and paint touch-up are included in your quote, and our team will confirm the details. Send us a photo of your old sign.

11. H2: Common Questions

What signs does an office need? 

Most offices need four groups: a brand sign at reception, room ID signs, wayfinding signs and directories, and the safety signs your building requires. Plan them as one system so fonts, finishes, and mounting heights match. Your building and fit-out team sets exit, stair, and evacuation signs.

Can name signs be changed when staff moves? 

Yes, when the sign has a replaceable name insert. Occupant names are exempt from the ADA sign rules, so you can change an insert while the room number stays in place. Send us the new name and our team will prepare a matching insert.

Do room signs need to be ADA compliant? 

Where room signs are provided, the ADA Standards require raised characters and Grade 2 Braille on signs that identify permanent rooms, such as room numbers, conference rooms, and restrooms, mounted 48 to 60 inches high on the latch side. Directional signs need visual rules only, and occupant names are exempt. See our ADA signs page.

How long does it take?

12. H2: Common Questions

How far ahead should I order for a show? 

Order as early as you can, and check your show's deadlines in the exhibitor manual. Plan time for the artwork check, production, and shipping to the advance warehouse, which can receive materials in the weeks before move-in. Graphics usually take one to two weeks and portable displays two to three, plus shipping.

Do you deliver to the Javits Center? 

We ship to the advance warehouse address in your exhibitor manual, or to your own address, with the labels your show provides. Javits rules and official contractors vary by show, so we do not promise direct delivery to the building. Send us your manual's shipping page, and we will confirm the options.

Can you update graphics on an existing display? 

Often yes. Send photos of your frame and its model number, and our team will check if new graphics fit. Swapping graphics lets one display work for the next show, and it keeps the frame you already own.

Rent or buy? 

Buy if you exhibit more than once, because a frame you own can take new graphics for each show. Renting suits a single large custom booth. We make displays and graphics you own, and our team can talk through a full custom booth if you need one.

What file format do you need? 

Send vector files (AI, EPS, or PDF) for logos and text, and photos at least 100 to 150 dpi at the final print size, in CMYK color. Bleed varies by display, so our team confirms the specs before printing. Images copied from websites print soft at large sizes.

Are your fabrics fire-retardant? 

Javits Center shows typically require fire-retardant fabrics and a certificate on request, and your exhibitor manual sets the details. Ask our team what we can supply for your display before you order. Check for a certificate before you assume any fabric meets a show's rules.

Vision Graphics makes custom trade show displays and graphics in Salt Lake City and ships them to your show, with an artwork check and one project lead from design to delivery. Your exhibitor manual covers labor, shipping, and venue services. Send your show name, dates, and booth size to start.
