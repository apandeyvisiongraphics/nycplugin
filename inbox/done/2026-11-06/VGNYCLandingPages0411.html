12. H2: Common Questions About Custom Signs in NYC

How much do custom signs cost in NYC? 

Most business signs cost between $300 and $20,000 installed. Window graphics sit at the low end, while illuminated channel letters and large building signs sit at the high end. NYC permits, access equipment, and landlord requirements can add cost. We itemize every quote from a photo or a site survey.

How long does a custom sign take? 

Simple graphics take one to two weeks. Lobby and office signs usually take two to three weeks. Illuminated exterior signs take three to five weeks, plus permit review. Landmark districts and building approvals can add time, so we flag them at the start of your project.

Do I need a permit for a business sign in NYC? 

Usually yes. New York City requires a permit for business signs over six square feet and for any illuminated sign. Attached signs can need an alteration permit, lit signs need an electrical permit, and landmark districts add review. Ask any sign company in New York you compare who files the paperwork. We prepare it and coordinate the licensed filer.

Who makes the sign? 

Our production team builds your sign to the drawing you approve, and we check each piece before it ships. Specialty items can involve trusted partners. We tell you before you approve the quote.

Who installs the sign? 

A licensed NYC sign hanger installs your sign, because the city requires one for most installations. A licensed electrician makes the final connection on lit signs. We tell you who will be on site before installation day.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance for our building? 

Yes. We provide a certificate of insurance on request, and we can send it before work begins. Buildings set their own coverage wording, so send us your requirements early, and we will match them where we can.

Can you install after hours or on weekends? 

Yes. We schedule installs around your building's rules, including evenings, weekends, and freight elevator windows. After-hours work can add labor charges, and we confirm that in your quote.

Can you remove our old sign? 

Yes. We remove the old sign as part of the install plan. NYC requires a licensed sign hanger for removal, and landmark buildings can require review. Any wall repair is scoped in your quote.

Can you handle signs for several NYC locations? 

Yes. We handle one address or many, with the same drawings, finishes, and schedule across every site. Tell us each building's address, and we confirm permits and installation for each one.

Do you serve all five boroughs? 

Yes. We serve all five boroughs, and every building has its own rules and access needs. Tell us your address when you request a quote, and we plan permits, scheduling, and installation for your site.

11. H2: Common Questions

Do you remove the old sign? 

Yes. Removal is part of the install plan, so the old sign comes down before the new one goes up. New York City requires a licensed sign hanger for most removals, and our licensed partners handle it. Send us a photo of your old sign, and we will plan both steps.

Do you repair the wall? 

Yes. Wall patching and paint touch-up are scoped in your quote, based on the photos you send. Old signs leave holes, marks, and adhesive, so the quote reflects your wall's condition. Tell us about your wall and any finish requirements.

Can you install after hours? 

Yes. We schedule installs around your building's rules, including evenings, weekends, and freight elevator windows. After-hours work can add labor charges, and we confirm them in your quote. Tell us your building's work hours when you request a quote.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance? 

Yes. We can provide a certificate of insurance upon request and send it before work begins. Buildings set their own coverage wording, so send us your requirements early. Our team will confirm what we can provide for your building.

Who does the installation? 

Licensed installation partners install your sign, and a licensed electrician connects lit signs. A Vision Graphics project lead manages the schedule, building access, and paperwork from start to finish. We tell you who will be on site before installation day.

How long does it take from approval to install? 

Simple graphics usually take one to two weeks, and lobby and office signs take two to three weeks. Illuminated exterior signs take three to five weeks, plus permit review. Shipping from our Salt Lake City facility adds transit time, which we confirm in your quote. Landmark districts and building approvals can add more.

Do you handle building management approval and permits? 

Yes. We prepare the drawings and paperwork building management asks for, and interior signs usually depend on that approval. Exterior signs generally need a New York City DOB permit, and lit signs need a separate electrical permit. Landmark districts add review. We coordinate the licensed filer.

Do you install in all five boroughs? 

Yes. We serve all five boroughs, and each building has its own access rules and approvals. Tell us your address when you request a quote, and we will plan the schedule, permits, and crew for your site.
