6. H2: Quick Answers

Do I need final artwork? 

No. Send your logo if you have one, or a photo of your old sign, and we will prepare a drawing for you to approve before anything is made. We accept HEIC, JPG, PNG, PDF, AI, and EPS files.

Can you quote from photos? 

Yes. Photos of the wall or storefront plus rough sizes are enough for a first quote. For lit and exterior signs, we may ask for a site survey before the final price.

How fast will you reply? 

We reply within one business day. If your project has a firm deadline, choose your timing in the form or tell us in the details box, and we will confirm what is possible.
