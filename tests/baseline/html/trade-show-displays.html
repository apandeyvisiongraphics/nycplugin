Planning for a Javits Center Show

Each Javits Center show sets its own rules in the exhibitor manual. Check yours before you order, and use this list as a start.

  • Deadlines: the advance warehouse window, the artwork due date, and your move-in time
  • Shipping: the advance warehouse address and shipping labels from your manual
  • Hand carry and labor: which setup work you may do yourself and which needs union labor
  • Fire-retardant materials: fabrics and decorations may need certificates
  • Island booths: designs and technical drawings may need to go to the venue
  • Hanging signs: rigging is arranged through the venue
  • Insurance: exhibitors may need general liability insurance

Vision Graphics makes the display and the graphics, and your exhibitor manual covers labor, shipping, and venue services.

Common Questions

How far ahead should I order for a show?+

Order as early as you can, and check your show's deadlines in the exhibitor manual. Plan time for the artwork check, production, and shipping to the advance warehouse, which can receive materials in the weeks before move-in. Graphics usually take one to two weeks and portable displays two to three, plus shipping.

Do you deliver to the Javits Center?+

We ship to the advance warehouse address in your exhibitor manual, or to your own address, with the labels your show provides. Javits rules and official contractors vary by show, so we do not promise direct delivery to the building. Send us your manual's shipping page, and we will confirm the options.

Can you update graphics on an existing display?+

Often yes. Send photos of your frame and its model number, and our team will check if new graphics fit. Swapping graphics lets one display work for the next show, and it keeps the frame you already own.

Rent or buy?+

Buy if you exhibit more than once, because a frame you own can take new graphics for each show. Renting suits a single large custom booth. We make displays and graphics you own, and our team can talk through a full custom booth if you need one.

What file format do you need?+

Send vector files (AI, EPS, or PDF) for logos and text, and photos at least 100 to 150 dpi at the final print size, in CMYK color. Bleed varies by display, so our team confirms the specs before printing. Images copied from websites print soft at large sizes.

Are your fabrics fire-retardant?+

Javits Center shows typically require fire-retardant fabrics and a certificate on request, and your exhibitor manual sets the details. Ask our team what we can supply for your display before you order. Check for a certificate before you assume any fabric meets a show's rules.

Where can I get custom trade show displays made in NYC for a Javits Center show?+

Vision Graphics makes custom trade show displays and graphics in Salt Lake City and ships them to your show, with an artwork check and one project lead from design to delivery. Your exhibitor manual covers labor, shipping, and venue services. Send your show name, dates, and booth size to start.
